# CERVEL Public Code of Conduct

CERVEL Public is a technical collaboration space. We want participation to be rigorous, respectful, and safe for contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected conduct

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and technical tradeoffs without attacking people;
- give specific, evidence-based feedback and assume good-faith participation unless there is clear reason not to;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, security-reporting boundaries, and intellectual-property restrictions;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, intimidation, or sustained disruption;
- stop unwanted contact when asked;
- keep public issues and pull requests focused on material appropriate for this public repository.

Disagreement is normal. Personal abuse is not.

## Public-repository safety

Do not post secrets, credentials, personal data, private communications, embargoed material, vulnerability details, or information taken from non-public CERVEL repositories or systems. If a discussion may contain sensitive security information, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of continuing in public.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate this code, repository security requirements, licensing obligations, or the public/private disclosure boundary. Maintainers may also restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the project.

Enforcement should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting the technical community rather than winning disagreements.

## Reporting conduct concerns

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainers privately through a repository-supported private channel when available. Do not expose personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to `SECURITY.md`.

## Scope

This code applies to participation in this repository and to conduct in project spaces where a person is representing CERVEL Public.
