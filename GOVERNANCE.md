# CERVEL Public Governance

This repository is the deliberately reviewed public developer surface for CERVEL. Governance here applies only to material intentionally published in this repository.

## Decision boundary

Maintainers decide what is accepted into the public surface. Publication of a schema, example, SDK type, document, or compatibility tool does not imply publication of related non-public implementation, architecture, infrastructure, data, protocols, or product behavior.

External participation is welcome, but contribution does not create a right to merge, roadmap control, access to private systems, or disclosure of non-public technology.

## Review principles

Maintainers evaluate proposed changes for:

- technical correctness and compatibility with published contracts;
- security and privacy impact;
- licensing and contributor authority;
- intellectual-property and disclosure risk;
- consistency with the public/private boundary;
- maintenance cost and long-term developer value.

Patent-relevant, security-sensitive, interoperability-defining, or disclosure-expanding changes may receive additional review or be declined even when technically sound.

## Public/private disclosure boundary

Publication is affirmative, not inferred. Contributors must not use this repository to reveal or reconstruct non-public CERVEL source, deployment details, private identifiers, customer information, credentials, unpublished protocol semantics, confidential research, or other restricted material.

When uncertain whether material is safe to publish, do not push it to a public branch. Raise the question privately with maintainers first.

## Contribution provenance

External contributions are expected to comply with `CONTRIBUTING.md`, the applicable license terms, and `DCO.md`. A DCO sign-off records contributor certification; it does not replace technical, security, disclosure, or IP review.

## Conduct and security

Participation is governed by `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`. Vulnerabilities and sensitive security matters must follow `SECURITY.md` and must not be disclosed through public issues or pull requests.

## Changes to governance

Governance documents may evolve as the public ecosystem grows. Material changes to contribution licensing, sign-off requirements, disclosure rules, or maintainer authority should be made explicitly through reviewed pull requests rather than through undocumented practice.
